## 一、美国（United States）

### EVID-001：美国无联邦层面DPP强制立法
- **Claim**: 美国目前没有联邦层面的数字产品护照（DPP）强制法规，被视为"DPP-adjacent"司法管辖区
- **Source**: Tilkal
- **URL**: https://www.tilkal.com/post/digital-product-passports-beyond-europe-how-global-markets-are-converging-toward-product-data-requi
- **Date**: 2026-06-16
- **Excerpt**: "The United States does not currently have a federal Digital Product Passport regulation equivalent to the European ESPR... many experts increasingly view the United States as a 'DPP-adjacent' jurisdiction."
- **Context**: 美国通过行业法规（食品安全、海关合规、强制劳动执法等）构建类似DPP的技术基础
- **Scope fit**: 政策法规维度
- **Confidence**: 高
